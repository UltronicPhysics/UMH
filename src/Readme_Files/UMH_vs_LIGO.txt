UMH Gravitational-Wave (LIGO) Simulation — README
===============================================

Purpose
-------
This simulation implements the Ultronic Medium Hypothesis (UMH) gravitational-wave
framework and applies it to the GW150914 event. The goal is NOT to compete with or
replace General Relativity (GR), nor to optimize waveform fits within GR-based
pipelines.

Instead, this simulation demonstrates that when cosmological *mapping assumptions*
(expansion, ΛCDM priors) are removed and replaced with a tension-based UMH mapping
(calibrated independently from Pantheon+), gravitational-wave observables
(frequency, phase, time dilation, amplitude) remain self-consistent across detectors
and produced a qualifying LIGO type observed Chirp.

The comparison is therefore with **GR pipelines**, not with GR itself.

Core UMH ingredients
--------------------

1) **Tension-based cosmological mapping (no expansion)**
   - UMH does not interpret redshift, time dilation, or amplitude scaling as arising
     from metric expansion.
   - A global **tension field** in a Lorentz-consistent medium governs the
     source–observer mapping.
   - This mapping is calibrated *once* from Pantheon+ supernova data and then
     transported unchanged to gravitational-wave observables.

2) **Forward UMH chirp generation (mechanism-first)**
   - The UMH chirp generator produces an intrinsic inspiral → merger → ringdown
     waveform from UMH medium dynamics.
   - No phenomenological waveform families are used.
   - No per-detector fitting, mass/spin maximization, phase warping, or template banks
     are involved.
   - The resulting waveform is a **forward prediction**, not a best fit.

3) **Frequency evolution**
   - The inspiral obeys the leading-order chirp scaling:
         df/dt ∝ f^(11/3),   f ∝ (t_c − t)^(−3/8)
   - This scaling is shared with GR at Newtonian order and reflects generic
     radiation-reaction behavior, not GR-specific ontology.
   - Higher-order PN terms may be included for diagnostic comparison only and are
     disabled in strict UMH demonstrations.

4) **Detector projection (geometry only)**
   - The intrinsic UMH waveform is projected into Hanford and Livingston using:
       • antenna pattern geometry,
       • a single global time reference,
       • a single polarity convention.
   - Relative detector timing is constrained by geometry; detectors are NOT fit
     independently.

Code components
---------------

A) UMH_Chirp_Generator.py
   - Generates the intrinsic UMH gravitational-wave strain.
   - Outputs time-domain strain, instantaneous frequency, phase, and metadata.
   - Visualization band limits (if present) are explicitly marked as VIZ-ONLY and do
     not affect waveform generation.

B) UMH_Ligo_Compiler.py
   - Loads publicly available LOSC strain data for GW150914.
   - Applies deterministic conditioning (bandpass, whitening) equally to data and
     UMH waveform.
   - Performs **diagnostic** comparisons only:
       • time-domain overlays,
       • spectrograms,
       • residuals,
       • windowed correlations.
   - No detector-specific tuning is performed in strict mode.

Pipeline philosophy (important)
-------------------------------

• This is NOT a matched-filter search.
• This does NOT maximize likelihood over waveform families.
• This does NOT claim superior statistical detection power.

Instead, the question being tested is:

   “If a physically real, Lorentz-consistent medium with a global tension law is postulated, 
    can that medium—using only its intrinsic dynamics and a single externally calibrated 
    mapping—generate the observed gravitational-wave phenomenology, without invoking metric 
    expansion, dark matter, or waveform-family optimization?”

GW150914 analysis steps
----------------------

1) **Load event data**
   - GW150914 strain data are loaded from LOSC.
   - A fixed event window is used (no sliding or optimization).

2) **Generate UMH waveform**
   - UMH chirp is generated independently of detector data.
   - No information from LIGO data feeds back into generation.

3) **Apply cosmological mapping**
   - Pantheon+-calibrated UMH tension mapping is applied to:
       • frequency evolution,
       • phase accumulation,
       • time dilation,
       • amplitude vs distance.

4) **Conditioning (symmetric)**
   - Identical bandpass and whitening procedures are applied to:
       • LIGO data,
       • UMH waveform.
   - Whitening uses Welch PSD with fixed parameters and a PSD floor.

5) **Projection & alignment**
   - One global time offset is applied.
   - One polarity convention is chosen.
   - No per-detector lag fitting or stretching in strict mode.

6) **Diagnostics**
   - Time-domain overlays and residuals.
   - Time–frequency spectrograms.
   - Windowed correlation metrics (reported, not optimized).

What to look for in the outputs
-------------------------------

Time-domain figures
-------------------
• Whitened UMH vs LIGO strain overlays for H1 and L1.
• Residual plots showing structure vs noise.
• Clear inspiral-merger-ringdown morphology alignment without per-detector tuning.

Spectrograms
------------
• Time–frequency ridges showing consistent chirp evolution.
• Residual spectrograms indicating where structure remains or vanishes.

Correlation diagnostics
-----------------------
• Windowed Pearson correlations computed under fixed rules.
• Values reported for transparency; not maximized over free parameters.

Key interpretation points
-------------------------

1) The GW150914 chirp does NOT uniquely force an expansion-based cosmology.
2) GR local dynamics remain valid; the disagreement is with **pipeline assumptions**.
3) A single, externally calibrated tension-based mapping can be applied consistently
   to both supernovae and gravitational waves.
4) The success of GR pipelines reflects descriptive flexibility, not necessarily
   unique physical ontology.

What this does NOT claim
-----------------------

• This is NOT a replacement for GR.
• This is NOT a detection pipeline.
• This is NOT a claim of higher SNR than LIGO templates.
• This is NOT a claim that GR waveforms are “wrong”.

It IS a demonstration that:
• the observed inspiral–merger–ringdown structure can arise from a medium-based mechanism
• cosmological interpretation is separable from local dynamics,
• current pipelines implicitly hard-code expansion + ΛCDM,
• UMH provides a coherent, mechanism-based alternative mapping.

Reproducibility & knobs
----------------------

• Strict mode:
    - No per-detector fitting
    - No stretch or lag optimization
    - One global alignment only

• Diagnostics only (reported, not applied):
    - Envelope-based event time estimate
    - Peak correlation lag
    - Optional PN-term comparisons

• All configuration flags and processing steps are written to JSON summaries for
  auditability.

Conceptual summary
------------------

GR and QFT describe observations.
UMH proposes the physical mechanism beneath those descriptions.

This gravitational-wave simulation shows that once the mechanism is specified,
multiple observational domains (SN redshift and GW chirps) can be treated
consistently without invoking expansion or dark matter.
