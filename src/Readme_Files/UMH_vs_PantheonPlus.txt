UMH_vs_Pantheon — README
========================

Purpose
-------
Evaluate whether the Ultronic Medium Hypothesis (UMH) in a non-expansion
cosmology reproduces the Pantheon+ SN Ia Hubble diagram using the full
STAT+SYS covariance, and compare against flat ΛCDM. UMH here has a single
effective cosmological degree of freedom (α), which is calibrated once at
low redshift and then held fixed. For the conditional final Hubble-diagram 
comparison, UMH has k=1 (the profiled intercept M), while flat ΛCDM has k=2
(the profiled intercept M plus fitted Ω_m).


Inputs
------
- Pantheon+ SH0ES catalogue: PantheonPlus_SH0ES.dat
- Pantheon+ STAT+SYS covariance: PantheonPlus_SH0ES_STAT_SYS.cov
- Analysis script: UMH_vs_PantheonPlus.py
- Theory reference: UMH.pdf
- Published paper: A. Dodge, "Pantheon+ and Redshift Observational Analysis of the UMH Redshift Formulation", Reports in Advances of Physical Sciences 8 (2026) 2650012, DOI 10.1142/S242494242650012X


Sample / cuts
-------------
- Pantheon+ SN-only sample: N = 1624 SNe after excluding the 77 Cepheid calibrators from the 1701-object table.
- Low-z calibration uses z ≤ 0.10.


Method (summary)
----------------
1) Low-z calibration
   - Regress distance d on L = ln(1+z) for z ≤ 0.10 using
     d = c0 + c1*L, with α = 1/c1.
   - Best α ≈ 2.481805e-04 1/Mpc (UMH native scale).

2) UMH (non-expansion) model
   - Cosmological redshift from the UMH clock-rate field.
   - Flux transmission factor:
         T(z) = exp[ –(β1 * L + β2 * L^2) ],  where L = ln(1+z).
   - In the conditional final Hubble-diagram likelihood, the absolute
     magnitude/intercept M is the only parameter profiled
     (analytic minimization of χ²(M)).
   - χ² computed with the full Pantheon+ STAT+SYS covariance (Cholesky).

3) ΛCDM reference
   - Flat ΛCDM with free Ω_m (shape) and profiled M (intercept); identical
     covariance handling.

4) Diagnostics
   - Residuals vs z and vs ln(1+z) with GLS trend tests.
   - Histogram of whitened residuals (should be ~N(0,1)).


Key results (this run)
----------------------
- Low-z slope: a = 2.481805e-04 1/Mpc.
- Best UMH (non-expansion): β1 ≈ 0.432, β2 ≈ –0.270, recovered in the
  Pantheon+ transmission-calibration stage and then held fixed in the conditional
  final Hubble-diagram likelihood; only M is profiled there.
  χ² = 1456.8 for N = 1624; counted parameters k = 1 (only M is adjusted
  and profiled) → dof = 1623.  AIC = 1458.8; BIC ≈ 1464.2.
- Best flat ΛCDM: Ω_m* ≈ 0.333, χ² ≈ 1457.0 (Ω_m free; M profiled).
  AIC = 1461.0; BIC ≈ 1471.8.
- GLS slopes (consistent with zero in all cases):
    • Residuals vs z:         m = 0.016 ± 0.023
    • Residuals vs ln(1+z):   m = 0.023 ± 0.032
- Whitened residuals: approximately standard normal.

Takeaway (model comparison)
---------------------------
The UMH conditional final-fit configuration, in which only M is profiled,
fits the Pantheon+ Hubble diagram comparably to flat ΛCDM, whose
conditional final fit varies Ω_m while also profiling M.
The near-identical χ² and absence of residual trends indicate that UMH
reproduces the observed SN distance–redshift relation under its
non-expansion redshift formulation.


Produced figures — what each shows
----------------------------------
1) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_Supernovae.png
   - Hubble diagram: distance modulus μ vs redshift z.
   - Blue points: Pantheon+ SNe with errors. Red curve: best-fit UMH.
   - Demonstrates that UMH reproduces the full dataset from z≈0 to z>2.

2) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_vs_LCDM.png
   - Same Hubble diagram with both UMH (solid red) and ΛCDM (dashed green).
   - Curves are visually indistinguishable across the full z range,
     illustrating comparable goodness-of-fit between the models.

3) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_vs_LCDM_Annotated.png
   - Overlay from (2) with shaded survey regimes (Low-z, SDSS, SNLS, HST).
   - Shows that the UMH/ΛCDM agreement persists across independent survey
     subsets, not just in aggregate.

4) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_Residuals.png
   - Residuals (data − model) vs z for the best fits.
   - Blue circles: residuals w.r.t. UMH; orange squares: w.r.t. ΛCDM.
   - Dashed black line at 0 and GLS trend lines.
   - Demonstrates no significant redshift-dependent bias for either model.

5) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_Residuals_Zoom.png
   - Zoom of residuals at high redshift (z ≳ 1.5).
   - Highlights that even at the highest z (where errors are larger) there
     is no systematic drift; UMH and ΛCDM remain consistent with the data.

6) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_Binned_Residuals.png
   - Mean residual per redshift bin with errors on the bin means.
   - Visual check for broad-scale structure: bin means scatter around zero,
     supporting an adequate global fit.

7) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_Residuals_vs_ln1pz.png
   - Residuals vs L = ln(1+z) with GLS trend.
   - Tests directly in the variable central to the UMH redshift law; slope
     consistent with zero, indicating no missing nonlinearity in L.

8) UMH_vs_PantheonPlus_WhitenedResiduals.png
   - Histogram of covariance-whitened residuals for UMH and ΛCDM.
   - Distributions are close to N(0,1), validating the covariance usage and
     that neither model leaves strong structure in the residuals.


How to reproduce
----------------
1) Ensure data/covariance paths in get_default_config() are correct.
2) Run UMH_vs_PantheonPlus.py (non-interactive; uses Agg).
   The script:
   - loads the catalogue and covariance,
   - performs the low-z calibration,
   - fits UMH (with only M as profiled in the final Pantheon+ fit),
   - fits a reference flat ΛCDM (Ω_m + profiled M),
   - writes a summary and saves the figures above.
